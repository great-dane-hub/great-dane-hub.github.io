03How the sizes are worked out

No black box

Rules of thumb for space and fit, shown in full. They do not replace trying the crate, bed or boot with your dog.

  1. Crate

    Inside length = nose to the base of the tail + the extra room. Inside height = floor to the top of the head + the extra room. The size class is the first of 36, 42, 48 and 54 inches that is at least that long.

  2. Bed

    Length = nose to the base of the tail + twice the extra room, so there is space to stretch out. Width = shoulder height + the extra room.

  3. Car boot

    Floor length = nose to the base of the tail + the extra room. Height to stand with the head lowered = shoulder height + the extra room; with the head up = head height + half the extra room.

  4. Blank fields

    Head height and length left blank are estimated as 1.3 × shoulder height, a rough ratio for the breed. The results say when an estimate is used. Measure for a closer fit.
